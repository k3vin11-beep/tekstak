# Keyword-targeted content: a practical guide (Premium)

**What a keyword is:** the words a customer types into Google. For a Pretoria bakery that might be
"custom birthday cakes Pretoria" or "cupcakes Arcadia". Keyword-targeted content means writing the pages and blog posts
around the real phrases your customer's customers use.

The template gives you the places to put them. The writing is the real work: use this worksheet with the client.

## Step 1: collect 10 to 20 phrases

- Ask the client: "If a stranger needed you, what would they type?"
- Type the main service into Google and look at the **suggestions** that drop down and the **People also ask** box.
- Look at what competitors' page titles say.
- Add the location: town, suburb, "near me" style phrases.

## Step 2: give each page ONE main phrase

| Page | Main phrase (example) | Where to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Home | custom cakes Pretoria | Google title, main heading, first paragraph |
| Services | cake decorating services Pretoria | Google title, main heading, service names |
| Pricing | wedding cake prices Pretoria | Google title, intro |
| Blog post | how much does a wedding cake cost | Post title, first paragraph, a heading |

Do not give two pages the same main phrase: they compete with each other.

## Step 3: fill in the SEO boxes

In the CMS every page has **Google title** and **Google description** (the file is `seoTitle` / `seoDescription`).

- **Google title:** about 50 to 60 characters. Main phrase near the front, brand at the end (the template adds the business
  name for you). Example: `Custom Cakes in Pretoria`
- **Google description:** about 150 to 160 characters. Say what you offer, where, and why choose you. Write it for a
  person, not a robot. It is the advert text under your link.

## Step 4: write for people first

- Use the phrase naturally. Never repeat it over and over ("keyword stuffing" can hurt).
- One H1 (main heading) per page. This is already built in.
- Answer real questions in the blog and FAQ page. Use the customer's own words.
- Add photos with a real description (alt text).
- Link related pages to each other (for example, a blog post linking to the Pricing page).

## Step 5: check results monthly

In Google Search Console (see `search-console.md`), open **Performance** and look at the searches that already show the
site. Write more about the ones that almost rank (positions 8 to 20).
