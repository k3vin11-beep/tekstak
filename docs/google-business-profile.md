# Google Business Profile: setup guide (part of the Standard package)

**What it does:** your Google Business Profile is the listing that appears on Google Maps and in the box on the right of
(or above) search results when someone searches for the business. For a local business it often brings in more
customers than the website itself. This is **not code**: you set it up at <https://business.google.com>.

## Steps

1. Sign in with the client's Google account and click **Add your business**.
2. Enter the **exact** business name, the way it appears on signs and invoices. Do not stuff keywords into the name
   (Google can suspend profiles for this).
3. Choose the **main category** carefully (for example "Hair salon" or "Plumber"). This matters more than anything else.
4. Add the address if customers visit, or set a **service area** if you travel to customers.
5. Add the phone number and the website address.
6. **Verify** the business. Google offers options such as phone, text, email, video or postcard. Which options appear
   depends on the business. Do not skip this: an unverified profile will not show up properly.
7. After verification, complete the profile:
   - Opening hours (and special hours for holidays)
   - A short description
   - Services or products with prices where possible
   - At least 5 good photos (shop front, work, team)
   - The **WhatsApp or appointment link** if the profile offers those fields

## Keep it consistent

Use the **same name, address and phone number** everywhere: the website footer, the contact page, Facebook and
Instagram. Mismatches confuse Google. In this template that information lives in one place
(`src/content/business.json`), so the website is always consistent.

## Ask for reviews

Reviews help rankings and trust. Once the profile is verified, copy its "Ask for reviews" link and send it to happy
customers on WhatsApp. Reply to every review, good or bad, politely.
