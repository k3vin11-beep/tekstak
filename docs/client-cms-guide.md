# How to edit your website (guide for the client)

You can change the words and photos on your website yourself. You do not need to know any code.

## Logging in

1. Go to **your website address followed by /admin/**, for example `www.yourbusiness.co.za/admin/`.
2. Click **Login with GitHub** and sign in with the GitHub account we set up for you.

## Changing text

1. On the left, open **Website content** and choose the page, for example **Home page**.
2. Click in a box and change the words.
3. Click **Publish** at the top (then **Publish now**).
4. Wait 1 to 2 minutes, then refresh your website to see the change.

## Changing or adding a photo

1. Open the page, find the photo box and click **Choose an image**.
2. Click **Upload** and pick the photo from your phone or computer. Then click **Choose selected**.
3. **Always fill in the "Describe the photo" box.** A short plain description such as "Chocolate cake with strawberries"
   helps people who use screen readers and helps Google understand your pictures.
4. Click **Publish**.

## Tips for good photos

- Use clear, bright photos. Landscape (wide) photos work best on the main picture.
- Very large photos are fine. The website shrinks them automatically so pages stay fast.

## Adding a new item to a list (services, projects on the Our work page, FAQ, pricing plans)

Scroll to the list and click **Add item** (or the + button). Fill it in and click **Publish**.
To remove an item use the small bin icon. To reorder, drag the item by its handle.

## Please do not

- Delete a whole page section by accident. If something goes wrong, tell us. Every change is saved and can be undone.

## Need something bigger?

New pages, new features or design changes are done by us. Send us a WhatsApp message.

## Writing a blog post (Premium)

1. Open **Blog posts** on the left and click **New blog post**.
2. Fill in the title, date, category, a short summary, the cover photo and the text.
3. Click **Publish**. Read `writing-blog-posts.md` for simple writing tips.

## The "Google title" and "Google description" boxes (Premium)

Each page has two optional boxes. They control how the page looks in Google search results.
Use the words your customers would search for, for example "Custom cakes in Pretoria". We will help you fill them in
the first time (see `keyword-guide.md`).
