A website that looks good but is hard to contact is like a shop with the door locked. Visitors want to ask one quick question and move on.

## Make it quick

Every extra step loses people. A short form with a name, an email and a message is enough. Do not ask for ten details before someone can speak to you.

## Meet customers where they already are

Many South Africans already use WhatsApp every day to talk to friends, family and businesses. A button or link that opens a WhatsApp chat feels natural and fast.

## Always give a second option

Some people prefer email, and some do not use WhatsApp at all. Show an email address next to the form so nobody is stuck.

## Reply quickly

A fast reply matters as much as the form. Tell visitors when to expect an answer, and then keep that promise.

## Track what works

With Google Analytics you can see how many people send the form or tap a contact link. If the numbers are low, change the wording or move the button, and check again next month.
