You do not need a huge website to get customers. You need the right things on it. Here is a simple checklist.

## 1. What you do and where

Say it in one sentence at the top of your home page: what you offer, and which area you serve. A visitor should understand your business in five seconds.

## 2. An easy way to contact you

Put your contact details where people can find them without searching. A contact form, an email address and a WhatsApp or phone link all help. The easier it is to reach you, the more messages you get.

## 3. Your services, with prices if you can

People hate having to ask for a price before they know if they can afford you. Even a "from" price helps them decide.

## 4. Real photos

Photos of your own work build trust far better than stock pictures. Describe each photo in a few words so people using screen readers, and Google, know what it shows.

## 5. Proof that you are real

Add reviews only if they are real, and ask customers before using their words. A short story about who you are also helps.

## 6. A clear next step

End every page with one thing you want the visitor to do: send a message, book, or call.

## 7. It must work on a phone

Most visitors will open your site on their phone. If you have to pinch and zoom to read it, they will leave.

Keep it simple, keep it honest and keep it up to date. That already puts you ahead of many small business websites.
