The right website depends on what your business needs it to do. Here is a simple way to choose.

## Landing Page (from R1,499)

Choose this if you just need to be online. One clean page with who you are, what you do and how to contact you. It takes about 2 weeks and is the quickest way to get started.

## Basic Website (R3,499)

Choose this if you have several services, want a gallery, or need separate About and Contact pages. You get up to 5 pages. We make any changes for you, so it suits businesses that rarely change their site.

## Standard Website (R5,999)

Choose this if you want to change your own text and photos without waiting for us. It includes an editing screen, a sitemap, optimised images and Google Business Profile setup help.

## Premium Website (R9,999)

Choose this if you want customers to find you on Google. It adds a blog, schema markup, speed tuning and tracking that shows how many people tap WhatsApp, call or send the form.

## Still not sure?

Start small. You can move up to a bigger package later as your business grows. Send us a message with a few details about your business and we will tell you which one fits.
