TeKStak Studios is a small web studio based in Pretoria. We build landing pages and business websites for small businesses and individuals who need to be found online.

We keep things simple. Every package has a clear price, a set time frame and a limited number of revision rounds, so you always know what to expect before we start.

We are also working on new services for businesses that need more than a website: **full-stack web applications** and **IT admin systems**.

- Clear prices and time frames
- Websites that work well on phones
- Help after launch with monthly maintenance plans
